# Approved package and promotion checkout

## Current proposal candidate — 25 November 2026

The current candidate adds verified passwordless guest access and delivery/collection choices using `20261125015757_checkout_collection_choice.sql`. Selected approved centre revisions and instructions are bound to authoritative quotes/orders; retries preserve the original frozen order. Only staff-approved no-additional-fee collection is supported. Existing package/voucher/stock/idempotency logic remains authoritative. Neither collection nor guest verification enables payments. Refunds/tax invoices/subscriptions/bonus-item campaigns remain missing; see [the handover](PROPOSAL-DELIVERY.md).

Drafts remain preparation records. A market administrator or compliance user with verified MFA must review the exact draft revision, select its published package SKU where applicable, enter an approval reference and acknowledge the checkout contract. Approval stores a separate immutable version. Editing a draft does not edit that approved version. Withdraw an existing package/voucher configuration before approving its replacement.

For packages, approval copies the proposed retail/member prices into market pricing without granting product registration or opening sales. Subsequent authorized market-price edits remain authoritative. Package composition is taken from the approved snapshot. Catalogue details show approved composition; checkout expands packages into physical components and aggregates overlaps with other packages and loose products. Component publication, country registration, quantity limits and stock are checked independently of the package SKU. Corporate stock is used; the separate third-party tenant inventory is unchanged.

Promotion contract version 1 is explicit: one voucher per order; a percentage or fixed discount applies once to its eligible-product subtotal, and its minimum subtotal applies to those same eligible products. Percentage amounts round down to whole minor units and honor any configured cap. Fixed discounts are limited to the eligible subtotal. The discount is apportioned across eligible commercial lines using exact integer arithmetic and deterministic remainder allocation. Customer/member audience, UTC validity window and configured total redemption capacity are checked by the database. This implements a supported engine contract, not invented RIWAY campaign values or approval of any real campaign. Automatic/stacked promotions, bonus-bottle redemption, membership qualification and free whole-order checkout remain separate scope.

Signed-in customers request `/api/checkout/quote`, review gross/discount/net amounts and send its hash with checkout. The order transaction rechecks authoritative pricing, approved versions, eligibility and physical stock. A stale quote fails for review; the existing idempotency key still returns the original frozen order after an uncertain attempt. Customer-supplied totals and stock decisions are never trusted.

Commercial order quantities and physical stock quantities are recorded separately. Stripe receives an exact net amount for each commercial selection (quantity one in the billing snapshot, with the purchased quantity in its description), including zero-priced eligible lines when the overall order is positive. The order and account history preserve actual purchased quantities and discounts. Zero-total orders are rejected explicitly, without an artificial minimum charge.

Voucher capacity is reserved transactionally with order creation, consumed by normal confirmed payment and released by confirmed cancellation/failure. Replays do not claim capacity again. Offer withdrawal stops new order creation; existing order and payment snapshots remain intact. Payment settlement compares reservations against the physical snapshot in both directions and consumes components exactly once. Open Stripe sessions retain existing reconciliation protections.

All market, product, sponsor, current-terms and payment-provider gates remain independent. This phase does not activate Stripe, email or Tax, supply real prices/stock/campaigns, implement shipping/invoices/returns or establish production acceptance. Real Stripe sandbox acceptance and authenticated UI acceptance remain required before commercial release.
